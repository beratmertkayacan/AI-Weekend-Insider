# Chickpea curry

Serves 4 · 30 min

## Ingredients
- 2 cans chickpeas
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 1 onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp curry paste
- 1 bag spinach
- rice to serve

## Steps
1. Fry onion and garlic, add curry paste for 1 min.
2. Add tomatoes, coconut milk and chickpeas; simmer 15 min.
3. Wilt in the spinach; serve over rice.
