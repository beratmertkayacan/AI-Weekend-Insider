# Karnıyarık

Serves 4 · 60 min

## Ingredients
- 4 eggplants
- 300 g ground beef
- 1 onion
- 2 tomatoes
- 2 green peppers
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- parsley

## Steps
1. Peel eggplants in stripes and fry or roast until soft.
2. Cook beef with onion, tomato and peppers.
3. Split eggplants, fill, top with tomato and bake 20 min at 180°C.
