# Bulgur pilavı with vegetables

Serves 4 · 30 min

## Ingredients
- 2 cups coarse bulgur
- 1 onion
- 1 green pepper
- 2 tomatoes
- 1 tbsp pepper paste
- butter

## Steps
1. Soften onion and pepper in butter, add pepper paste.
2. Add tomatoes and bulgur, then 3 cups hot water.
3. Cover and cook 15 min; rest 10 min before serving.
