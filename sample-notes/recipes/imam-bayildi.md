# İmam bayıldı

Serves 4 · 70 min

## Ingredients
- 4 eggplants
- 3 onions
- 4 garlic cloves
- 3 tomatoes
- parsley
- olive oil
- 1 tsp sugar

## Steps
1. Fry or roast eggplants until soft.
2. Slowly cook onions and garlic, add tomatoes, parsley and sugar.
3. Fill eggplants, add a little water and bake 30 min; serve at room temperature.
