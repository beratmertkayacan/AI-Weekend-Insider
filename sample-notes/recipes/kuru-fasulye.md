# Kuru fasulye

Serves 6 · 90 min

## Ingredients
- 2 cups dried white beans (soaked overnight)
- 1 onion
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp pepper paste
- olive oil
- rice to serve

## Steps
1. Soften onion in oil, stir in both pastes.
2. Add drained beans and hot water to cover; simmer until tender.
3. Serve with rice pilaf and pickles.
