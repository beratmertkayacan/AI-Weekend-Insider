# Beef tacos

Serves 4 · 25 min

## Ingredients
- 500 g ground beef
- 8 small tortillas
- 1 onion
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 tomatoes
- 1 lettuce
- cheddar
- 1 lime

## Steps
1. Brown beef with onion and spices.
2. Chop tomatoes and lettuce, grate cheese.
3. Warm tortillas, fill and finish with lime.
