# Pasta pomodoro

Serves 4 · 20 min

## Ingredients
- 500 g spaghetti
- 1 can whole tomatoes
- 3 garlic cloves
- olive oil
- fresh basil
- parmesan

## Steps
1. Cook pasta in salted water.
2. Warm sliced garlic in olive oil, add crushed tomatoes, simmer 10 min.
3. Toss pasta with sauce, basil and parmesan.
