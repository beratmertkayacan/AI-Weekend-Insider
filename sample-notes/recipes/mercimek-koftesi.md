# Mercimek köftesi

Serves 6 · 40 min

## Ingredients
- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 cup fine bulgur
- 1 onion
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 bunch spring onions
- parsley
- lettuce leaves
- 1 lemon

## Steps
1. Cook lentils in 3 cups water until soft, stir in bulgur off the heat.
2. Fry onion with tomato paste and knead into the mix with herbs.
3. Shape into fingers; serve in lettuce with lemon.
