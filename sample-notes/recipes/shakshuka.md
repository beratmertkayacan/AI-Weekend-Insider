# Shakshuka

Serves 2 · 25 min

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 1 red pepper
- 1 onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- bread to serve

## Steps
1. Soften onion, pepper and garlic; add spices.
2. Add tomatoes, simmer 10 min.
3. Crack in eggs, cover and cook until just set.
