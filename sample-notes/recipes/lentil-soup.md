# Lentil soup (mercimek çorbası)

Serves 4 · 35 min

## Ingredients
- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 onion
- 1 carrot
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 lemon
- 6 cups water or stock

## Steps
1. Soften chopped onion and carrot in oil, stir in tomato paste.
2. Add rinsed lentils and water, simmer 25 min.
3. Blend smooth, season with cumin; serve with lemon.
